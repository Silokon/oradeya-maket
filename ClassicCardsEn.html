Frequently asked questions

How is consultation time counted?+

Time is tracked by the server, not the browser. Reloading the page does not reset the timer, and if the connection drops the consultation is paused.

Can I send photos and videos?+

Yes, you can send photos up to 10 MB and videos up to 100 MB and up to 3 minutes long. Files are kept in private storage that only the consultation participants can access.

What happens to unused minutes?+

A package is valid within the current consultation. If you end the conversation yourself, the remaining time is not carried over. If a consultation was interrupted for technical reasons, support will look into it.

Is payment secure?+

Payments are processed by Stripe, card details never reach the service's server.

Do I need to sign up?+

Yes, a consultation requires an account with a confirmed email. This keeps your conversation history and free minutes.

Does it replace a psychologist or a doctor?+

No. Consultations are for information purposes and do not replace medical, psychological or legal help.
